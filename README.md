## ⚡ A little about me

In 2024, I decided to forge my own path in software development, embracing self-directed learning to break with convention. What began as a personal goal is now a solid reality.

At 21, I combine my degree in Computer Systems Engineering with over two years of professional experience as a Junior Frontend Developer at Actosoft. During this time, I've led key web projects from planning to deployment, working closely with multidisciplinary teams to create modern, optimized, and accessible interfaces. My goal remains the same: to put technology at the service of people in a humane and efficient way.
